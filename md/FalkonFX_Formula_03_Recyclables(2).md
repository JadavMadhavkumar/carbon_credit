**3. Recyclables Carbon Credit Formulas**

Recycling credits are calculated on a material-substitution basis: the
credits represent CO₂e that would have been emitted producing virgin
material instead of using recycled feedstock. Metals earn the highest
credits because primary production is extremely energy-intensive.

**3.1 Universal Recycling Formula**

<table>
<colgroup>
<col style="width: 100%" />
</colgroup>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td><p><strong>Recycling Credit</strong></p>
<p><strong>C_rec = W × (EF_virgin - EF_recycle) × η × M × V × L ×
Q</strong></p></td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

| **Variable**   | **Unit**   | **Description**                                 |
|----------------|------------|-------------------------------------------------|
| **C_rec**      | kg CO₂e    | Credits from recycling pathway                  |
| **W**          | kg         | Weight of material submitted                    |
| **EF_virgin**  | kg CO₂e/kg | Emission factor of virgin material production   |
| **EF_recycle** | kg CO₂e/kg | Emission factor of recycled material processing |
| **η**          | 0.0–1.0    | Recycling efficiency at processing facility     |
| **M**          | 0.8–6.0    | Credit multiplier (material-specific)           |
| **V**          | 0.50–1.00  | Verification score                              |
| **L**          | 0.90–1.10  | Locality factor                                 |
| **Q**          | 0.60–1.00  | Quality / purity factor                         |

| PET Plastic (1) | 3.10 | 1.32 | 1.78 | 0.82 | 2.1 |

| Mixed Plastics | 2.20 | 1.10 | 1.10 | 0.60 | 0.8 |

| Cardboard | 1.40 | 0.51 | 0.89 | 0.85 | 1.1 |
| Clear Glass | 0.85 | 0.54 | 0.31 | 0.95 | 0.8 |
| Coloured Glass | 0.85 | 0.54 | 0.31 | 0.90 | 0.7 |

**3.3 Aluminium Detailed Formula**

Aluminium has the highest multiplier (6.0×) because primary smelting
uses ~14 kWh of electricity per kg.
